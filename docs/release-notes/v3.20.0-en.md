# CC Switch v3.20.0 (S1)

> This is the first batch of the product-upstream `farion1231/cc-switch` `v3.19.2..v3.20.0` sync into the Web-first fork. S1 ships the docs, preset and i18n churn: the new PPIO, JieKou AI and XycAi partner presets across all supported apps, the `xycai` partner promotion in every locale, the RunAPI domain migration to `runapi.host` (with `runapi.co` kept as a fallback endpoint), the removal of the legacy partner star badge from the main panel provider cards, and the version bump to 3.20.0. The standalone Web-server deployment model, dual-runtime parity, and the existing Web hardening are unchanged; the desktop updater remains disabled and no authentication was introduced.

## Highlights

- **New partner presets**: PPIO, JieKou AI and XycAi are registered across Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, OpenClaw, Hermes (and Gemini for XycAi), each with a brand icon and, where supported, endpoint fallbacks.
- **RunAPI domain migration**: the RunAPI preset now points at `runapi.host` as its primary domain across all six apps, with the previous `runapi.co` retained as an `endpointCandidates` fallback on Claude Code, Codex and Grok Build.
- **Cleaner main panel**: the legacy partner ⭐ badge is removed from the main-panel provider cards; partner stars live only in the preset selector, and the now-unused `officialPartner` translation key is dropped from every locale.
- **Version bump**: `package.json`, `Cargo.toml`, `tauri.conf.json` and `Cargo.lock` advance to `3.20.0`.

## Fork-Specific Deviations

- The `zh-TW` locale and `README_DE` are not restored; upstream hunks touching them were dropped as no-ops.
- The `claudeDesktopProviderPresets` surface and the desktop-only sponsor banner tables in the READMEs remain excluded, so upstream's desktop-preset and README-partner hunks (FennoAI offer update) were dropped as no-ops.
- The `codexChatReasoning` field on the PPIO/JieKou Codex presets is **not** introduced here — it belongs to the per-model reasoning-levels work in a later batch and the fork's `CodexProviderPreset` type does not yet carry it.

## Out of Scope (Later Batches)

The remaining v3.20.0 sync work — security/data-integrity fixes, pricing/usage seeds, codex/provider features, UI/refactor, Windows adaptation, CI adaptation, and the three large new features (Pi native agent, Codex Alpha WebSearch, managed OAuth accounts) — lands in subsequent batches S2–S8 and dedicated subtasks.
